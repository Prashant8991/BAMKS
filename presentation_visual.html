☁️ OFF-CHAIN: Encrypted Cloud Storage

  • Raw AES-256-GCM Ciphertext: Unreadable binary payload.
  • 96-bit Nonce & 128-bit GCM Tag: Integrity seal.
  • Encrypted Inverted Index: Hashed discrete log tokens $g^{\eta \cdot H_1(kw)}$.
  • Why Off-Chain? Prevents high Ethereum gas fees for large medical images.

⛓️ ON-CHAIN: Ethereum Smart Contract

  • `deletedDocRegistry[docId]`: Boolean revocation mapping.
  • Public Verification Tag $y_k$: $y_k = g^{\sigma_k} \bmod P$.
  • Transaction History: Block timestamp & sender address.
  • Why On-Chain? Guarantees immutability & $\mathcal{O}(1)$ revocation in 0.001 ms!
