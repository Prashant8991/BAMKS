Department: Surgical Oncology & Pathology Laboratory

Immunohistochemistry Biomarkers:

- Progesterone Receptor (PR): Positive (75%, moderate)
- HER2/neu: 3+ Overexpressed (Positive)
- Ki-67 Proliferation Index: 45%

Conclusion: Grade 3 Invasive Ductal Carcinoma, Triple-Positive (ER+/PR+/HER2+). Referred to Tumor Board for neoadjuvant chemo-immunotherapy.
