🛡️ Cryptographic Security Foundations

The platform implements the mathematical framework of BAMKS (Cheng et al., Elsevier Internet of Things, 2026), fortified with our fine-grained dynamic single-document protocol:

1. Multi-Owner CP-ABE Access Control
Access tokens $\Phi$ are generated collaboratively across multiple hospital data owners. Plaintexts are encrypted under access trees (e.g., Role=Doctor AND Dept=Cardiology) using bilinear maps $e: G \times G \to G_T$.
2. Conjunctive Multi-Keyword Trapdoors
Searches execute over encrypted inverted indices without exposing keywords to the cloud. Trapdoor tokens $(T_1, T_2, T_3)$ allow conjunctive matching in constant round trips.
3. On-Chain Verifiable SNIZK Proof (RVSC)
The Cloud Storage Provider produces an aggregated Schnorr Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge proof $(R, \hat{\pi})$. The Result Verification Smart Contract verifies proof correctness on Ethereum in real time.
⚡ Research Gap & Proposed Protocol Innovation
Base Paper Limitation (Cheng et al., Section 8, Conclusion):
"In the future, we plan to extend BAMKS to support time-sensitive data sharing with adding, deleting, and inserting operations."
In the base paper, all documents are locked into a single version $F_{ver}$. Updating 1 document forces re-calculating master version keys ($\delta_{ver}, \xi_{ver}$) and re-indexing all $L=1000$ files ($O(L \times m)$ complexity = 5,560 ms).
Our Dynamic Protocol Solution:
• SingleDocAdd: Independent single-file index encapsulation. Bounded to $O(m)$ (0.99 ms).
• SingleDocDelete: Ethereum mapping flag deletedDocRegistry[docId] = true. Bounded to $O(1)$ (0.0001 ms).
• On-Chain RVSC Audit: RVSC filters revoked files during zero-knowledge proof verification.
